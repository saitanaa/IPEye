Please note that information is not always accurate!

Why may the information be incorrect?

Here are the reasons:

- The API: The API can sometimes provide incorrect information to IPEye.

- The Internet Service Provider (ISP): The ISP can sometimes be imprecise on several pieces of information, such as the location of the IP, the connected device and the use of a VPN .

- The target: The target may modify certain information, which may result in incorrect data.
